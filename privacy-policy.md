This Privacy Policy explains how {{ site.title }} ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or play our games, apps, and related services (together, the "Services"). {{ site.title }} is a project of [{{ site.parent_company.name }}]({{ site.parent_company.url }}).

By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you contact us (for example by email), we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.

**Information collected automatically.** Depending on the game or platform, we or our service providers may collect:

- Device information such as device model, operating system, language, and a device or advertising identifier.
- Gameplay and usage data such as sessions, progress, in-game events, and crash reports.
- Approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region).
- Purchase records for in-app purchases. Payments are processed by the app store or platform; we never receive your full payment card details.

**This website.** This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking on this site. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit; see [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain our games and save your progress.
- Fix bugs, prevent crashes, and improve performance and gameplay.
- Process and verify in-app purchases.
- Show advertising in games that include it, and measure its effectiveness.
- Respond to your questions and support requests.
- Detect and prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Third-party services

Our games may use third-party services such as app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam, and others), analytics and crash-reporting tools, cloud hosting, and advertising networks. These providers may collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies. Where a game uses such services, the relevant providers will be listed in that game's store listing or in-game settings where required.

## Advertising

Some of our games may display ads. Ad partners may use device identifiers to show ads and measure performance. Where required by law or platform rules, we will ask for your consent before using personalized advertising. You can also limit ad tracking through your device settings (for example "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Delete advertising ID" on Android).

## Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

- With service providers who help us run the Services, under obligations to protect it.
- When required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
- As part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case you will be notified as required by law.

## Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Gameplay and analytics data is generally kept in aggregated or pseudonymous form.
