Last Coat Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 (children and ages)

Last Coat is a puzzle game made by Mod Studio (modstudio.games). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Last Coat has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress (finished levels, stars, hints, streak and settings) and your answer to the age question are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads

Last Coat shows two kinds of ads, both provided by Google AdMob: a short ad between some puzzles (the one-time Remove ads purchase turns these off), and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for an extra hint or extra undos. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies
  • information about your device and the app (model, OS version, language)
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes

For players under 13 this is limited, see Children below. This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

Purchases

Last Coat offers one optional purchase, Remove ads, which turns off the ads between puzzles. It is sold and processed by Google Play under Google's payments privacy notice. We never see your card or payment details. The game only keeps a note on your device that ads are removed, and asks Google Play when it starts whether your account owns the purchase.

Children

Last Coat is made for players of all ages, including children. The first time it starts, the game asks the player's age and keeps the answer only on the device. For players under 13, every ad request is marked as directed to children under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy: those players only see non-personalised ads suitable for all ages, and Google does not use their advertising ID to personalise ads or build profiles. Players under 16 are not shown the European consent form and only get non-personalised ads there. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent can reset the age answer by clearing the game's data or reinstalling it.

Your choices

  • Rewarded ads only play when you tap a "Watch an ad" button; every level can be played without them. The ads between puzzles can be removed with the one-time purchase.
  • To delete your game data, use Settings, Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it.
  • For data Google holds about you, use your Google account's privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
